# The 24 Documented AI Writing Patterns (WikiProject AI Cleanup Standards)

Large language models leave distinct linguistic fingerprints. This reference catalogs the 24 documented patterns of "AI Slop" and the humanized counter-measures.

---

### 1. Significance Inflation
* **Pattern:** Elevating minor updates into monumental events ("This marks a pivotal milestone in our collective journey").
* **Fix:** State the actual action simply: "We shipped the update yesterday."

### 2. Throat-Clearing Openings
* **Pattern:** Broad philosophical stage-setting ("In today's fast-paced digital world, developers are constantly seeking...").
* **Fix:** Delete paragraph 1 entirely. Start *in media res* with an error or action.

### 3. Hallmark Wrap-ups
* **Pattern:** Moralizing, saccharine conclusions ("Let us embrace innovation and build a brighter tomorrow together").
* **Fix:** Conclude with a practical heuristic or a genuine question.

### 4. Overly Symmetrical Three-Part Lists
* **Pattern:** Rigid triplets where each item has the exact same sentence structure and syllable count.
* **Fix:** Break the symmetry. Use two items, or one short item followed by a detailed paragraph.

### 5. Forced Antithesis ("Not just X, but Y")
* **Pattern:** Constant contrasting formulas ("It's not just a feature; it's a philosophy").
* **Fix:** Delete the antithesis. Say what it actually does.

### 6. Robot Verbs
* **Pattern:** *Delve, elevate, foster, harness, leverage, showcase, spearhead, navigate, underscore, transcend.*
* **Fix:** Use plain physical verbs: *dig into, use, build, show, ran, dealt with, proved.*

### 7. Robot Nouns
* **Pattern:** *Tapestry, testament, realm, beacon, synergy, plethora, symphony.*
* **Fix:** Use concrete objects: *mix, proof, domain, tool, collection.*

### 8. Robot Adjectives
* **Pattern:** *Pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, meticulous, groundbreaking, vibrant, multifaceted, seamless.*
* **Fix:** Use grounded terms: *key, big, careful, fast, clean.*

### 9. Autopilot Transitions
* **Pattern:** Formal academic signposts: *Furthermore, moreover, subsequently, in light of this, it is worth noting.*
* **Fix:** Connect thoughts using natural logic, conjunctions (*And, But, So*), or zero transition.

### 10. Em-Dash Overuse
* **Pattern:** Stacking multiple em-dashes (—) in every single sentence to simulate deep thought.
* **Fix:** Limit to 1 em-dash per entire post, or use natural commas and parentheses.

### 11. Low Sentence Burstiness (Uniform Length)
* **Pattern:** Every sentence averages 15 to 20 words with uniform clause structures.
* **Fix:** Mix 2-word fragments with 25-word narrative compound sentences.

### 12. Lack of Natural Contractions
* **Pattern:** Writing "do not", "cannot", "we have", "it is" like a legal affidavit.
* **Fix:** Write "don't", "can't", "we've", "it's".

### 13. Passive Agentless Voice
* **Pattern:** "Optimization protocols were implemented to facilitate latency reduction."
* **Fix:** "I deleted 3 queries and shaved 200ms."

### 14. Fake Rhetorical Questions
* **Pattern:** "Have you ever wondered how top engineering teams achieve optimal results?"
* **Fix:** State the direct insight or observation without pretending to be a late-night TV infomercial.

### 15. Superlative Stacking
* **Pattern:** Piling praise on ordinary work ("an extraordinary, unparalleled breakthrough").
* **Fix:** Remove praise; let the metrics speak for themselves.

### 16. Eerie Emotional Neutrality
* **Pattern:** Complete absence of frustration, fatigue, excitement, or doubt.
* **Fix:** Add human friction: "I was annoyed," "It was embarrassing," "I wasn't sure it would work."

### 17. Lazy Engagement Bait CTAs
* **Pattern:** Ending with "Agree? Thoughts? What do you think?"
* **Fix:** Ask a targeted question about the reader's real experiences.

### 18. Abstract Concept Bloat
* **Pattern:** Discussing "scalability, maintainability, and paradigms" without concrete tools.
* **Fix:** Name the exact database table, API route, or library.

### 19. Unnatural Parallelism
* **Pattern:** Every bullet point beginning with identical gerunds (*"Optimizing...", "Enhancing...", "Accelerating..."*).
* **Fix:** Write bullets like casual developer checklists.

### 20. Corporate PR Disguise
* **Pattern:** Announcing product features like a press release.
* **Fix:** Frame as a builder sharing what they just hacked together.

### 21. Syllable Inflation
* **Pattern:** Using "utilize" instead of "use", "methodology" instead of "method", "facilitate" instead of "help".
* **Fix:** Choose the simplest word that delivers the meaning.

### 22. Absence of Mid-Thought Self-Correction
* **Pattern:** AI never changes its mind or hesitates.
* **Fix:** Add self-correction: "Or at least, that was the theory until staging crashed."

### 23. Missing Sensory Details & Timestamps
* **Pattern:** Generalized time ("recently", "over time").
* **Fix:** Specific timestamps: "at 6:45 PM on a Friday," "after 9 hours of debugging."

### 24. Predictable Comma Cadence
* **Pattern:** Introductory adverbial phrase + comma + independent clause in every sentence.
* **Fix:** Vary sentence openings: start with a verb, a noun, a conjunction, or a single number.
