# The Humanizer Pre-Flight Checklist

Before hitting publish on any social post, run this 5-point editing audit:

---

## 1. The "Out-Loud Test"
Read the draft aloud at a conversational speaking pace.
- Did you run out of breath anywhere?
- Did any sentence sound like a line from a software brochure?
- Would you say this exact sentence to a friend at a diner?

---

## 2. The Burstiness Scan
Look at the length of each sentence visually on the screen:
- [ ] Is there at least one sentence under 5 words?
- [ ] Is there at least one compound sentence carrying a longer thought?
- [ ] Are sentences varying between short, medium, and long?

---

## 3. The Anti-Slop Word Hunt
Scan and purge:
- [ ] No *delve, elevate, foster, harness, leverage, showcase, spearhead, navigate*
- [ ] No *tapestry, testament, beacon, realm, synergy, plethora*
- [ ] No *paramount, pivotal, crucial, vital, meticulous, groundbreaking*
- [ ] No *furthermore, moreover, subsequently, in light of this*

---

## 4. The Friction & Vulnerability Check
- [ ] Is there at least one moment where things were messy, confusing, or broken?
- [ ] Are there exact numbers or metrics (e.g., ms, $, %, rows, lines)?
- [ ] Does it sound like a builder sharing lessons rather than an executive lecturing?

---

## 5. The Mobile Formatting Check
- [ ] Hook is under 140 characters before line breaks.
- [ ] No paragraph exceeds 2 sentences.
- [ ] Generous whitespace between thoughts.
- [ ] Closing question invites peer battle-scars rather than generic "Agree?".
