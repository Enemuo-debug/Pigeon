# The Anti-AI Field Guide: Writing Without The Robot Accent

When large language models generate LinkedIn posts or essays, they default to statistical predictability (low perplexity) and uniform sentence length (low burstiness). The result is "AI Slop"—smooth, pleasant, completely forgettable, and instantly flagged by modern readers.

This guide catalogs the exact markers of AI-generated prose and details the surgical human replacements.

---

## 1. The Prohibited Vocabulary (Dead Giveaways)

If any of these words or phrases appear in your draft, delete them immediately. They are cognitive fingerprints of language models.

### A. The "Robot Verbs"
| AI Word | Why It Fails | Human Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Delve** | Nobody in real life says "let's delve into." | Dig into, look at, examine, pull apart, unpack |
| **Elevate** | Corporate marketing jargon. | Level up, sharpen, boost, improve, make better |
| **Harness / Leverage** | Overused tech pitch speak. | Use, run with, tap, apply |
| **Foster** | Sounds like an HR memo. | Build, grow, encourage, spark |
| **Showcase** | Sterile museum language. | Show, demo, highlight, reveal |
| **Spearhead** | Resume inflation word. | Led, ran, started, kicked off |
| **Navigate** | Vague metaphor for just doing work. | Figure out, handle, deal with, work through |
| **Underscore** | Academic commentary. | Proves, highlights, drives home, makes clear |
| **Transcends** | Over-dramatic and theatrical. | Goes beyond, beats, cuts through |

### B. The "Robot Adjectives & Nouns"
| AI Word | Why It Fails | Human Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tapestry** | "A rich tapestry of..." is 100% LLM signature. | Mix, collection, combination, mess |
| **Testament** | "A testament to the hard work..." | Proof of, shows how, because of |
| **Crucial / Vital / Paramount** | Inflated urgency that means nothing. | Key, big, important, essential, needed |
| **Meticulous** | Artificial praise. | Careful, painstaking, thorough |
| **Game-changer** | Empty hype word. | Huge shift, big deal, changed everything |
| **Groundbreaking** | Hype word unless curing diseases. | New, radical, first of its kind |
| **Ever-evolving / Dynamic** | Lazy throat-clearing fluff. | Fast-changing, messy, shifting |
| **Beacon / Symphony / Realm** | Poetic fluff out of place in engineering/business. | Cut entirely. |

### C. The "Robot Transitions & Signposts"
* ❌ *Furthermore / Moreover / In addition* $\rightarrow$ Use **And**, **Plus**, or just start the next sentence.
* ❌ *Consequently / Subsequently* $\rightarrow$ Use **So**, **Then**, or **As a result**.
* ❌ *It is worth noting that / It is important to remember* $\rightarrow$ Delete the phrase; state the point directly.
* ❌ *At its core / Ultimately* $\rightarrow$ Cut. State the realization.
* ❌ *In conclusion / To summarize* $\rightarrow$ Never use in a social post or essay.

## 2. Structural Giveaways: Breaking AI Symmetry

### The "Rule of Three" Trap
LLMs compulsively organize everything into three neat, symmetrical items:
- Item 1 with 12 words and an adjective.
- Item 2 with 12 words and an adjective.
- Item 3 with 12 words and an adjective.

**The Human Fix:** Break the rhythm. Give one point 3 words. Give the next point a 2-sentence mini-story. Give the third point a single blunt fragment.

### The "Throat-Clearing" Opening
LLMs love to set the stage before saying anything:
- ❌ *"In today's fast-paced tech landscape, developers are constantly looking for ways to streamline their workflow..."*
- ✅ *"I broke production at 6:45 PM on a Friday."*

**The Rule:** Cut the entire first paragraph of an AI draft. Start *in media res* (in the middle of the action).

### The "Cheesy Hallmark" Wrap-up
LLMs always try to wrap up with an inspirational ribbon:
- ❌ *"Let us embrace the journey, continuously iterate, and strive to create a brighter future together!"*
- ✅ *"Anyway, don't forget to sanitize your inputs. What's the weirdest bug you chased this month?"*

**The Rule:** Human endings are grounded, conversational, curious, or self-deprecating. Never lecture the reader on becoming a better human being.

## 3. Sentence "Burstiness": The Rhythm of Human Speech

AI text maintains an eerily uniform cadence: sentences average 16–20 words, every sentence has one dependent clause, and commas appear at mathematically predictable intervals.

Human speech is **erratic and syncopated**:

```text
Short sentence. 
Another short one.
Then a longer, winding sentence that carries an observation across two thoughts, 
dragging the reader forward until they finally hit the period.
And then—a punch.
```

### The Cadence Test:
1. **Count the words in 4 consecutive sentences.** If they are all 15–20 words, rewrite two of them immediately.
2. **Read the text out loud.** If you cannot say it comfortably to a friend sitting across from you at a diner without feeling like an actor in a software commercial, it is too artificial.

## 4. Specificity over Abstraction (Grounded Friction)

Humans think in sensory details, dollar amounts, specific tools, and raw friction. Language models think in conceptual summaries.

| Abstract AI Version | Grounded Human Version |
| :--- | :--- |
| "We optimized our infrastructure to reduce latency significantly." | "We killed 14 redundant Redis calls and dropped our p99 latency from 840ms to 92ms." |
| "Building a startup involves unexpected hurdles and constant learning." | "We had 5 customer discovery calls yesterday. 4 said our pricing was insulting and 1 hung up after 2 minutes." |
| "Effective communication is essential for engineering leadership." | "Stop sending 4-paragraph Slack essays when a 30-second screen recording answers the question." |
