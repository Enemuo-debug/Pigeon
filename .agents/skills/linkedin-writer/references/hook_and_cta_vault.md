# Hook and CTA Vault: Battle-Tested Openers & Closers

The first 2 lines of a LinkedIn post determine whether a user clicks "...see more" or keeps scrolling. The closing line determines whether they comment or leave.

Here is a vault of formulas and tested patterns.

---

## Part 1: High-Retention Hook Formulas

### 1. The High-Contrast Reality Check
* Formula: `[Thing A] is hard. [Thing B] is harder. Choose your [X].`
* Formula: `Everyone thinks [Action A] causes [Outcome]. In reality, [Surprising Factor] caused 90% of it.`
* Formula: `We spent [Time/Money] building [Feature]. Exactly [Small Number] people used it.`

### 2. The Vulnerable Metric / Build-in-Public Hook
* Formula: `Week [N] of building [Project]. We lost [Customer/Revenue] today.`
* Formula: `Our revenue is at [$X]. Down [$Y] from last month. Here's why I'm not panicking:`
* Formula: `I just pushed commit #[SHA] to production. It breaks our oldest architectural assumption.`

### 3. The "Expensive Mistake" Post-Mortem Hook
* Formula: `I made a mistake that cost our engineering team [Days/Dollars/Morale].`
* Formula: `A single missing [Config/Index/Condition] crashed our API for 4 hours on Saturday.`
* Formula: `Why we just deleted [X%] of our codebase—and why I wish we did it 6 months ago.`

### 4. The Contrarian Tech Hook
* Formula: `Stop using [Trendy Tool/Pattern] for [Common Task]. You are burning time.`
* Formula: `Most [Role, e.g. Senior Dev] advice in 2026 is dead wrong. Here is what actually moves the needle:`
* Formula: `The fastest way to slow down an engineering team is [Accepted Best Practice].`

### 5. The "How I Went From X to Y" Narrative Hook
* Formula: `6 months ago, I couldn't get [Problem] to work. Today, it handles [Impressive Stat]. Here's the exact diff:`
* Formula: `How a 12-line bash script replaced a $400/month SaaS tool for our team:`
* Formula: `I spent 40 hours auditing our production logs. Here are 4 things I found that shocked me:`

---

## Part 2: The Rules of the First 2 Lines

1. **Keep it under 140 characters before the break:** LinkedIn truncates text after the first 2–3 lines on desktop and mobile.
2. **Never open with a pleasantry:** Banish "Happy Monday everyone!", "Excited to share that...", "Hope you are doing well."
3. **Put the conflict or punchline immediately in line 1.**
4. **Line 2 should deepen the curiosity gap.**

---

## Part 3: Low-Friction Call-to-Actions (CTAs)

Avoid awkward, demanding CTAs like *"Agree? Share your thoughts below and hit repost!"*
Use authentic, conversational prompts that invite peer-to-peer reflection.

### Community Battle-Scar CTAs:
* *"What is the weirdest production bug you've ever had to debug on a weekend?"*
* *"What's a tech stack decision you made early that came back to haunt you?"*
* *"Anyone else find that writing in public forces you to stop lying to yourself?"*
* *"What would you add to this checklist?"*

### Low-Friction Feedback CTAs:
* *"If you've tackled [X], how did you handle [Y]?"*
* *"Drop your take below: Are you a fan of [Tool A] or [Tool B] for this?"*
* *"Curious how other teams handle this. Do you enforce [Rule] in CI/CD?"*

### Subtle Resource / Demo CTAs:
* *"The repository is open source. Drop a comment if you want the link to the PR."*
* *"I put together our exact checklist in a GitHub gist. Let me know if you want it."*
