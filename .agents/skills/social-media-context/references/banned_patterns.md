# Banned Patterns & Vocabulary Bank

This document lists the forbidden words, sentence patterns, and structural cliches that immediately break the author's voice and trigger the "AI generic" filter.

---

## 1. Absolute Blacklist (Zero Tolerance)

| Category | Forbidden Words & Phrases |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Robotic Verbs** | *delve, elevate, foster, harness, leverage, showcase, spearhead, navigate, underscore, transcend, demystify* |
| **Robotic Nouns** | *tapestry, testament, beacon, realm, synergy, plethora, symphony, catalyst, watershed moment* |
| **Robotic Adjectives** | *paramount, pivotal, crucial, vital, meticulous, vibrant, multifaceted, unprecedented, groundbreaking, game-changer, seamless, bespoke* |
| **Autopilot Transitions** | *furthermore, moreover, subsequently, in light of this, it is worth noting, needless to say, at its core, ultimately, in conclusion* |
| **Fake Enthusiasm Openings** | *I am thrilled to announce, I am excited to share, Big news!, Happy Monday everyone!* |
| **Lazy CTAs** | *Agree? Thoughts? What do you think? Let me know in the comments!* |

## 2. Structural Patterns to Ban

1. **Forced Antithesis / "Not Just X, But Y":**
   - ❌ *"It's not just about writing clean code; it's about empowering humans."*
   - ✅ *"Clean code just makes debugging on Monday less painful."*

2. **The 3-Item Symmetry Trap:**
   - LLMs compulsively create lists of exactly three items with identical syllable counts.
   - Break symmetry: use two items, or four items with uneven lengths.

3. **Passive Agentless Verbs:**
   - ❌ *"A decision was made to refactor the module."*
   - ✅ *"I decided to tear down the module."*

4. **Significance Inflation:**
   - ❌ *"This update marks a transformative milestone in our engineering odyssey."*
   - ✅ *"We deployed the patch yesterday."*
